Eric Schmidt Warns Against a 'Manhattan Project for AGI'

Eric Schmidt and AI experts warn against a U.S. "Manhattan Project" for Artificial General Intelligence (AGI), similar to nuclear programs. In their paper, they suggest a strategy called Mutual Assured AI Malfunction (MAIM) to prevent international tensions and cyberattacks, recommending healthier control over AI tech.

Sources: Business Insider, Times of India, TechCrunch

Pentagon Taps AI for Faster Military Decisions

The Pentagon's Thunderforge project is using AI for quicker military planning. With help from Scale AI and Microsoft, it aims to improve decision times and simulate outcomes. While promising, this raises issues of AI trust and ethical challenges.

Sources: BGR, The Defense Post, Breaking Defense

Alibaba's New AI Model Boosts Stock 8%

Alibaba's QwQ-32B AI model has boosted their stock by 8%. Built on the Qwen2.5-32B framework, it excels in math and coding, heating up the AI race with the U.S. It's available on Alibaba's Qwen Chat and on Hugging Face.

Sources: Dig.Watch, InfoWorld, Alibaba Cloud

OpenAI Launches GPT-4.5

OpenAI's latest model, GPT-4.5, brings better understanding and intelligence, reducing mistakes. Available to higher-tier ChatGPT Pro users, it's more advanced in recognizing patterns and emotions.

Sources: TechCrunch, PureAI, Axios

AI Designing Futuristic Materials

AI is changing the game in material science! With tools like Microsoft's MatterGen and USC's DRAGONS, it is creating super strong and light materials. These advances are crucial for planes and cars, promising a future of efficient, cost-friendly inventions.

Sources: Live Science, Materials Square, USC Viterbi School
